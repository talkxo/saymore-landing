# Saymore — hold a button. say the thing.

**Saymore** types your words — formatted, spelled right, in your tone —
wherever your cursor is. It all runs on your Mac. No cloud. No telemetry.

▸ **[Download the alpha](https://talkxo.github.io/saymore-landing/Saymore.zip)**
— one click. unzip → drag to Applications → hold ⌥ Space.

## Why it's different

- **Hold to talk** — like a walkie-talkie. Escape chickens out.
- **Better Formatting** — casual in Messages, proper grammar in email.
- **Learns your words** — a name it mishears once, it fixes forever.
- **Your media behaves** — music dips while you speak, comes right back.

## The honest numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| **22 MB** | the entire app. fits through any door. |
| **~1 GB** | speech models, downloaded once — offline forever. |
| **0 bytes** | of audio or text uploaded. not once. not ever. |

## Requirements

Apple Silicon (M1+) · macOS 14 or newer · free while in alpha
