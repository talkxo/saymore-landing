THE BORING (GOOD) STUFF

The nerdy bits, for the record.

MADE FOR APPLE SILICON

Native Swift, menu-bar first, deeply integrated into macOS. 22 MB, no Electron, no bloat.

SPEECH → TEXT

Parakeet v3 by NVIDIA — tops the Open ASR Leaderboard. Its TDT decoder runs ~1,000× faster than real time, beating Whisper on accuracy.

BETTER FORMATTING

Powered by S1 mini by Superwhisper — tiny, purpose-built, best in class at a fraction of the 4–6 GB models other apps lean on. The rules we wrap around it never rewrite your words — they only get the formatting right. Every time.

ON-DEVICE ENGINE

Both models run through Core ML on Apple's Neural Engine, compiled for Apple Silicon. Fastest results, lowest memory, easiest on the battery.

ONE-TIME SETUP

The two models above — the brain files, ~1 GB — download once from Hugging Face. Then fully offline — flight mode ✈️ included.

PRIVACY FIRST

Audio, text, and history never leave your Mac. No telemetry, no tracking, no account.

ICLOUD SYNC

Completely optional. Your custom dictionary syncs through the iCloud account on your Mac — built for multi-Mac setups. Nothing in between. No us.
